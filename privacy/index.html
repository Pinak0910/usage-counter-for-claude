Privacy Policy

Usage Counter for Claude · Effective November 3, 2026

Usage Counter for Claude shows your Claude usage limits next to the claude.ai chat box. Everything it reads stays in your own browser. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data.

What the extension reads

  • Your usage figures from claude.ai: the percentage used for each limit, when each limit resets, and extra usage credits spent and your monthly cap, if you use them.
  • Your Claude organization ID, read from claude.ai's own lastActiveOrg cookie or account list, so it can ask claude.ai for the right account's usage.
  • When a reply finishes. The extension notices that a reply has finished streaming so it can update your usage. It does not read, decode or store the text of your messages or Claude's replies.

What it does not collect

  • It never reads, stores or sends your conversations, prompts, files or replies.
  • It never stores or sends your cookies or password. From claude.ai's cookies it picks out only the organization ID, and it does not run on any other website.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location or browsing history.

Where your data goes

The extension talks only to claude.ai, using the login you already have, to fetch the usage figures above. That is the same information the Usage page in claude.ai settings shows you. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Usage figures, your organization ID and your settings are saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your device, so the line can show them instantly. They are removed when you uninstall the extension.

Notifications

If you keep alerts on, the extension shows Chrome notifications when you reach 80% or 90% of a limit, hit a limit, or a limit resets. These are created on your device and contain only your usage figures. You can turn them off in the extension's popup.

Sharing and sale

Your data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to judge creditworthiness. No person reads it.

The use of information received by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
